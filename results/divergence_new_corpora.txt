==============================================================================
WHY KAPPA IS 0.000 ON THE NEW CORPORA, AND WHAT THAT DOES NOT MEAN
==============================================================================

kappa = (observed - chance) / (1 - chance)

corpus                observed   chance   kappa  heur bands  ssvc bands
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
kubernetes-goat         0.2805   0.1895   0.112           3           4
kube-prometheus         0.3333   0.3333   0.000           1           3
microservices-demo      0.0000   0.0000   0.000           1           1
new pooled              0.0952   0.0952   0.000           2           4

THE MECHANISM. If one rater emits a single band L for every
finding, then observed agreement is just the share of findings
the OTHER rater also called L, and chance agreement is
1 x that same share. The two are identically equal, so kappa is
exactly 0 no matter how the other rater behaves. It is not a
measurement of disagreement; it is what the formula returns when
one margin is degenerate.

One or both raters emit a single band on: kube-prometheus, microservices-demo.

microservices-demo is the extreme case: the heuristic returns Track* for
all 15 findings and SSVC returns Track for all 15. Observed and chance
agreement are both 0. The two engines in fact order those findings
IDENTICALLY - they rank them all equal - and differ by a constant
one-band offset. kappa reports that as no agreement at all.

new pooled reaches 0.000 by a DIFFERENT route. Neither rater is constant
there (2 heuristic bands, 4 SSVC bands), and its observed agreement of
0.0952 simply happens to equal its chance agreement exactly. That is a
coincidence of these particular marginals, not the degenerate case,
and it would not survive a change to the corpus mix. Its weighted
kappa of 0.231 is non-zero, which is the clearer reading: the
disagreements are all one band wide rather than arbitrary.

This is a limitation of the MEASURE, not a result about either
engine. Hardened, professionally maintained manifests are exactly
the homogeneous case that triggers it: few rules fire, the
findings that do are alike, and the heuristic collapses to one or
two bands. Goat, being deliberately vulnerable, has spread in
both raters and its kappa of 0.112 is meaningful.

WHAT STILL WORKS, AND WHAT DOES NOT:
  corpus                finding kappa  rule-set  rule-set wtd  rules fired
  ------------------------------------------------------------------------
  kubernetes-goat               0.112     0.250         0.412           11
  kube-prometheus               0.000     0.000         0.000            4
  microservices-demo            0.000     0.000         0.000            2
  new pooled                    0.000     0.077         0.333            6

Rule-set kappa does NOT rescue a single-band corpus. Collapsing
to one row per rule removes corpus composition, but it cannot add
variance to a rater that has none, so it is 0.000 on both new
corpora individually and recovers only when they are pooled.

The divergence-cause breakdown DOES remain informative throughout.
It does not depend on either margin having spread: it asks, per
finding, which counterfactual would close the gap, and that
question has an answer even when every finding is identical.
That is what the section below reports.

==============================================================================
THE TWO NEW CORPORA DIVIDE CLEANLY BY MECHANISM
==============================================================================

  kube-prometheus
      floor 6.0 in band Attend
      remediation-pricing 4/4 (100%)
  microservices-demo
      floor 5.3 in band Track*
      band-floor-mismatch 15/15 (100%)

  No cause appears in both. The split is total: one corpus is
  explained entirely by how the heuristic prices remediation, the
  other entirely by the two scales' ranges failing to line up.
  The floor is what separates them - 6.0 sits in Attend, so SSVC's
  Act and Track* verdicts are both reachable and the gap is priced;
  5.3 sits in Track*, so SSVC's Track is below everything the
  heuristic can emit and no single-factor change could close it.

  DIRECTION INVERTED FROM GOAT:
    corpus                heuristic higher  ssvc higher
    ---------------------------------------------------
    kubernetes-goat                     26           33
    kube-prometheus                      2            2
    microservices-demo                  15            0
    new pooled                          17            2

  On Goat the two engines were close, with SSVC ranking higher
  slightly more often. On the new corpora the heuristic ranks
  higher almost always. Which engine is more urgent is therefore
  a property of the corpus, not a fixed property of the methods.
