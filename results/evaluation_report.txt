CVSS-style scoring and the SSVC tree, and exist on any corpus. The fifth,
band-floor mismatch, is a range mismatch specific to what a corpus produces.

corpus pattern: the divergent findings come from 6 rules; the largest single contributor is K8S-SEC-006 (33 findings).
Both engines score on the rule id alone, so all of a rule's findings diverge together - rules that fire in bulk dominate the pattern.
The 23 band-floor-mismatch findings above and the empty Track row of the agreement matrix are the same fact seen twice: the heuristic's lowest score on this corpus is 5.30, so it never uses Track at all, while SSVC assigns it freely. Those findings could not have agreed no matter what any single factor said.
